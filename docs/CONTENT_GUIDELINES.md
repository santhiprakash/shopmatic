# Content Guidelines for shopmatic.cc

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-03

---

## Purpose

shopmatic.cc is a platform for influencers and affiliate marketers to curate and share product recommendations. To maintain trust and comply with legal requirements, we have established these content guidelines.

---

## What You Can Share

### Allowed Products

- **Physical Products**: Electronics, fashion, home goods, beauty products, sports equipment, etc.
- **Digital Products**: Online courses, software, ebooks, digital templates, subscriptions
- **Services**: SaaS products, membership sites, consulting services
- **Products from Any Platform**: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, AppSumo, or any other legitimate marketplace

### Content Requirements

- Products must be genuine and legally sold
- Descriptions must be accurate and not misleading
- You must have the right to promote the product (affiliate status or genuine recommendation)
- Adult products must include proper age verification notice

---

## What You Cannot Share

### Strictly Prohibited

1. **Illegal Products or Services**
   - Drugs, weapons, stolen goods
   - Services that facilitate illegal activities
   - Counterfeit or replica products misrepresenting as authentic

2. **Hate Speech or Violence**
   - Products promoting hate speech, discrimination, or violence
   - Items associated with extremist ideologies

3. **Adult Content Without Verification**
   - Adult products without proper age verification mechanisms
   - Explicit content involving minors

4. **Fraudulent or Deceptive Products**
   - Get-rich-quick schemes
   - Fake reviews or testimonials
   - Products that make false claims

5. **Products Violating Platform Terms**
   - Items that violate Amazon Associates Program terms
   - Products from platforms that prohibit affiliate promotion

---

## Automated Moderation

Our system automatically checks:

1. **Domain Blocklist**: Products from known bad domains are rejected
2. **Profanity Filter**: Titles and descriptions are checked for inappropriate language
3. **Flagged Terms**: Certain terms trigger manual review

If your product is flagged, you'll see an error message explaining why. You can:

1. Modify the product details to comply with guidelines
2. Contact support if you believe there was an error

---

## Reporting Violations

If you see content that violates these guidelines:

1. Click the "Report" button on any product
2. Select the violation type
3. Provide additional details if requested

Reports are reviewed by our moderation team within 24-48 hours.

---

## Consequences of Violations

| Violation Type | First Offense | Repeated Offenses |
|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Minor (profanity in description) | Warning | Product removed |
| Moderate (misleading claims) | Product removed | Account suspension |
| Severe (illegal products) | Account termination | Legal referral |
